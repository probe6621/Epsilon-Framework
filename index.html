1. The Fallacy of Point Zero

Modern field theories encounter structural failures—ultraviolet divergences, black hole singularities, and mathematical blow-ups—because they integrate spatial manifolds down to an unphysical mathematical abstraction: absolute point zero (\(r = 0\)).

$$\frac{\partial\epsilon}{\partial r} \neq 0 \quad \implies \quad \epsilon > 0$$

Replacing point-singularities with an irreducible \(\epsilon\)-boundary condition eliminates unphysical infinite blow-ups at the structural level. Artificial mathematical renormalization is replaced by natural geometric containment.
